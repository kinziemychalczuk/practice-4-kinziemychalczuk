Piet Mondrian

Dutch pioneer of abstract art, who developed from early landscape pictures to geometric abstract works of a most rigorous kind.

Born in Amersfoort, Utrecht. Studied painting at the Amsterdam Academy 1829-4 and again, part time, 1896-7.

Friendship with the painter Simon Mans and painted landscapes in the Hague School tradition.

1872 - Born in Amersfoort, the Netherlands

1912 - Moves to Paris and sees Cubism

1917 - Co-funds De Stijl with Theo van Doesburg

1930 - Paints Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

1944 - Dies in New York

"I don't want pictures. I just want to find things out."

For Piet Mondrian, abstract painting was the means of achieving an equilibrium between "concrete" (the tangible and specific aspects of reality percieved by the senses) and the "universal" (the underlying, essential truths that he believed were constant and unchanging).
